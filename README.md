# Mobile Sales Data Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard designed to analyze mobile phone sales performance, customer purchasing behaviors, transaction trends, and geographical distribution across India.

---

## 📌 Features & Visualizations

* **KPI Cards:** Overview of Total Sales (769M), Total Quantity (19K), Total Transactions (4K), and Average Order Values (40K).
* **Geographical Sales Map:** City-wise distribution of total sales across Indian metropolitan hubs (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, etc.).
* **Sales by Mobile Brand & Model:** Tabular and bar chart analysis tracking performance across top brands (Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, Xiaomi) and specific models (e.g., iPhone SE).
* **Transaction Insights:** Payment method distribution (UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Cash) broken down via pie chart.
* **Temporal Trends:** Monthly sales volume trends (Line Chart) and day-of-the-week sales patterns (Area Chart).
* **Customer Ratings Breakdown:** Funnel visualization of ratings (1 to 5 stars) across orders.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Filters for Month (January–December), Mobile Model, Payment Method, Brand, and Day Name.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation & Modeling:** Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* **Data Source:** Mobile Sales Transaction Dataset (CSV/Excel)
